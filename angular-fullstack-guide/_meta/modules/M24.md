### 6. Resolvers and route data: `ResolveFn`, failures and router resources · delta 850 · SEC-M24-06
- **Problem:** a product page should not render empty and then jump; a missing product should land on a not-found page with the URL the user typed; a slow resolver freezes the app with no feedback; a resolver that returns `EMPTY` silently cancels the navigation.
- **Model:** a **resolver** (`ResolveFn<T>`) runs after every guard has passed (F15) and before activation; the router takes its **first value** and puts it into the route's `data` under its key. No value (`EMPTY`) cancels with `NoDataFromResolver`; an error fails the navigation (`NavigationError`, the promise rejects) unless `withNavigationErrorHandler` `[Added in v15.2]` turns it into a redirect; a `RedirectCommand` redirects. **Router resources** (`resources` with `withRouterResources()` `[Developer preview since v22.2]`) define `resource`s per route; blocking by default, `nonBlocking()` to activate first. When guards and resolvers rerun is set per route by `runGuardsAndResolvers` and globally by `onSameUrlNavigation`. ### 8. Preloading strategies and route reuse · delta 800 · SEC-M24-08
